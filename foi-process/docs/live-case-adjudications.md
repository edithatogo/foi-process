# Independent live-case adjudications

This record documents two independent reviews of public Alaveteli captures. It
is governance and provenance evidence, not a legal opinion and not a public
request-level publication artifact. The conclusions are limited to the
captured evidence listed below.

## Review protocol

- Two reviewers worked independently from the repository captures.
- The review question was whether the public record supports a process-level
  conclusion about response, timeliness, and substantive completeness.
- Platform labels such as `successful` were treated as observations, not as
  proof of statutory compliance.
- No requester identity, correspondence beyond the captured public material,
  or new source material was inferred.

## Case 2: FYI request 10

- **Authority:** Department of Conservation
- **Title:** Value of Crown mining royalties
- **Public source:** <https://fyi.org.nz/request/value_of_crown_mining_royalties_2>
- **Observed timeline:** request 2010-03-22; response 2010-05-11
- **Material facts:** The public record identifies a response and one PDF
  attachment for a request covering four information categories. No extension
  event is visible in the captured timeline.
- **Response review:** Visual inspection of the one-page scanned PDF confirms
  that DOC addressed all four requested categories individually. It stated
  that the first category was not held (OIA s 18(e)), the second was partly
  publicly available and otherwise not held (ss 18(d) and 18(e)), and the
  third and fourth did not exist (s 18(e)).
- **Process conclusion:** Substantive coverage is supported, subject to the
  accuracy of DOC's section 18 grounds. The letter is dated 2010-05-05 while
  the captured response event is 2010-05-11; the request was sent
  2010-03-22. No extension event is visible, so timing remains potentially
  delayed or unexplained. The evidence does not establish final statutory
  compliance.
- **Confidence:** High that a response and attachment were issued; medium for
  apparent delay; low-to-medium for completeness and legal compliance.
- **Unresolved:** The PDF is image-only and was visually reviewed rather than
  OCR-verified; no extension notice, delivery confirmation, or independent
  statutory-source check of the cited section 18 grounds is captured.
- **Attachment digest:**
  `735b4e4d25faa2228891abdd0b68573b4db2bd9253e6cdd3f34e3452151fdbde`
- **Capture evidence:**
