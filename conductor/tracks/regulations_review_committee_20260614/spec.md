# Specification: Regulations Review Committee Proceedings Ingestion

## Overview
The Regulations Review Committee has a distinct constitutional role in New Zealand, examining regulations (delegated legislation) and hearing complaints against them. This track builds a specialised pipeline to gather, parse, and structure reports, complaints, and official proceedings from this committee.

## Scope & Features
1. **Targeted Data Extraction:** Extract proceedings and reports specifically tagged or authored by the Regulations Review Committee from parliamentary sources.
2. **Complaint Analysis Parsing:** Structured capture of complaints, reporting the specific regulations challenged and the committee's findings (e.g. invalid delegation, breach of civil liberties).
3. **Linkage to Regulations:** Cross-reference committee findings directly against target regulations in the secondary legislation corpus.
