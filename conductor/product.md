# Product

Build an evidence-preserving, Rust-first process-intelligence layer for archived and live FOI/Alaveteli corpora. FYI.org.nz is the first implementation profile, not a hard-coded scope boundary.

Success means a capture can be replayed deterministically into FOI-O-aligned events and OCEL, mined by Rust4PM, checked by evidence-backed conformance profiles, and explored safely in Propel/Hugging Face without treating inferred legal outcomes as certified facts.
